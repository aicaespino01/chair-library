# Uchida EPO (エポ): two-tone, no arms

**Identified: Very likely (model). Version: unsure.** The Office Busters item code `UCHSNAEPO` decodes as Uchida, swivel with no arms, EPO. Google Lens matched it to Uchida EPO listings (Kagg.jp "EF-400 5-340-3025 ウチダ EPO" and a used "エポEF2-600 ブルー"). Uchida's own page shows the same two-tone back/seat style. **Not yet known:** exact code and back height. **To confirm:** ask Office Busters for a photo of the label under the seat.

## At a glance
- Uchida's basic office chair. Uchida Yoko is a Japanese office-furniture maker that has been in business for over 100 years (Kagg.jp).
- "Free synchro" recline: back and seat tilt together, said to be a first in its class when launched.
- Long seat-height range with a low lowest point (Uchida), shown as 42.5–53.3 cm on one shop listing.
- No arms, no headrest, no lumbar adjustment, no seat-depth slide listed.
- Cheap new in Japan (about ¥26,000 to ¥29,000), so less of a bargain used than the Okamura chairs.

- Free synchro recline and tension dial at a basic-chair price.
- Long height range with a low minimum (helps shorter users).

- Spare parts available from Uchida in Japan.

## Cons
- Used, age unknown. Warranty unknown.
- No arms, no headrest, no lumbar adjustment, no seat-depth slide.
- Fabric back: warmer than mesh.
- Few independent reviews.
- Cheap new in Japan, so the used saving is smaller.
