# Kokuyo M4 (CR-G2201): leaf green, white see-through back shell, fixed arms

**Identified: Very likely.** The back of the chair has a frosted, see-through white shell with vertical slits and a KOKUYO badge. That is the look of Kokuyo's M4 "white shell" chair, and Kokuyo sold the M4 in leaf green. Fixed arms point to the CR-G2201 family. **To confirm:** photo of the label under the seat (should start CR-G2201 or CRS-G2201), or check for the seat-height button on the armrest.

## At a glance
- Kokuyo's standard M4 task chair (about 2013 onward), now discontinued in Japan.
- **Self-adjusting rocking:** the back stiffens or softens by your weight, so there is no tension knob to fiddle with.
- **Seat-height button on the armrest**, so you can adjust it while seated.
- Breathable leaf-green mesh fabric on back and seat; see-through white shell looks light.
- Fixed loop arms (help you stand; may stop you sliding fully under a desk).
- No seat-depth slide listed; seat height range not found yet.
