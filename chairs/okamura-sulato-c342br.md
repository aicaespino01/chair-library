# Okamura Sulato (C342BR): low back, design arms, hanger, gray

**Identified: Very likely.** A photo of the label under the seat shows the Okamura logo, the "sulato" manual card, and the code **C342BR**. C342 is the Sulato low back with fixed "design arms" and a hanger. The letter "B" is not in any shop list; Okamura uses "B" for a gray shell on its Escudo chairs, which matches the gray shell in the photo. **To confirm:** a clearer photo of the label.

## At a glance
- Same Sulato seat and recline as the armless C331 (see the C331ZR file), plus fixed arms and a coat hanger.
- Comfortable seat with 3 firmness zones; 20° ankle-tilt recline that locks anywhere.
- Lowest seat 42.0 cm: a bit high for people around 150 cm.
- Label year starts "2021", so this one may be fairly new.
- New in Japan about ¥90,800 (black shell version).
