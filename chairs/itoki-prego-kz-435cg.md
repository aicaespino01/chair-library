# Itoki Prego (KZ-435CG): high back, loop arms, blue

**Identified: Very likely.** A photo of the whole chair matches Office Busters Japan's photos of the Prego high back KZ-435CG (rounded fabric back, thick gray loop arms, gray plastic base). The label under the seat shows the Itoki logo, JOIFA 602 and a blurry code that fits "KZ-435CG-W?N?". **To confirm:** a sharp, close photo of the label.

The same label photo was first saved as "Itoki epios KE-450" (2026-11-14). That was a misreading: the whole-chair photo shows a different back, so the epios file was removed.

## At a glance
- Itoki's mid-priced task chair, high back version, with fixed loop arms.
- Rocking with "Hip Tilt": the rear of the seat tilts with the back. Rocking strength knob.
- Seat height 41.0–53.0 cm: short users may need a footrest.
- No seat-depth slide, no lumbar adjustment.
- Seen at Office Busters PH; price not stated.
