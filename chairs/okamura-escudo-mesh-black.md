# Okamura Escudo mesh, black, aluminium base

**Identified: Very likely.** The manual card under the seat says "okamura / ESCUDO mesh SERIES" (エスクードメッシュシリーズ), and the chair has the Escudo mesh look: a mesh back that curves in at the bottom, adjustable arms, and a slide lever under the seat. This is a **different colour version** from the yellow-green Escudo already in the library ([okamura-escudo-mesh.md](okamura-escudo-mesh.md)): black mesh, black shell and a polished aluminium base. **To confirm:** photo of the Okamura label under the seat (product code), or measure the overall height (low back about 93–103 cm, high back about 102–111 cm).

## How to identify it
**Already known:** Okamura Escudo mesh series (manual card under the seat), black, adjustable arms, aluminium base.
**Still needed:** the exact product code (low back or high back) and year.
1. Ask the shop for one photo of the **whole underside of the seat**, close enough to read the white Okamura label (code starting with C4..). A shop sticker (for example OKASWAESR...) also helps.
2. Codes C491 = low back; C497 or C498 = high back. Or ask them to measure the chair from the floor to the top of the back at its lowest seat height: about 93 cm = low back, about 102 cm = high back.

## At a glance
- Same chair series as the yellow-green Escudo already in the library, in black with a polished aluminium base.
- **Seat-depth slide (50 mm)** and **fully adjustable arms** (height, angle, front/back).
- Breathable mesh back with a flexing inner shell; ankle-tilt recline that locks anywhere.
- Lowest seat 42.2 cm: a bit high for short users without a footrest.
- About ¥118,000 to ¥132,000 list price new in Japan.

## Pros
- Seat-depth slide and 3-way adjustable arms: fits many body sizes.
- Breathable mesh back; no PU or faux leather.
- Polished aluminium base (sturdier and nicer than plastic).
- Wide seat (50.0 cm).

## Cons
- Lowest seat 42.2 cm: short users may need a footrest.
- No lumbar adjustment, no headrest.
- Discontinued line (no new manuals after 2021): spare parts may be harder to find.
- Aluminium base may show corrosion spots in salty, humid air over time.
