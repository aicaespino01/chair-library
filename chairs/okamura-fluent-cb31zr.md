## Warranty and returns
- Okamura: 1 to 3 years in Japan (Rakuten listing for this model, matching Okamura's JOIFA-based terms). Okamura's Sulato manual excludes used chairs and use outside Japan; the Fluent manual is over 10 MB and couldn't be read, so this isn't confirmed for Fluent but is very likely the same.
- Office Busters: exchange only if proven defective within 3 days. Free storage 7 days, then ₱500 a day. (OB storage and exchange policy)

## Shop claims not confirmed by the maker
- "Height:80cm x Width:50cm": the maker's lowest height is 88.2 cm (low back) or 94.2 cm (high back).
- "Nylon mesh type backrest": Okamura says mesh but doesn't name the fiber on the pages read.
