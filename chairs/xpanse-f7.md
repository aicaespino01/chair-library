## Pros
- New, with adjustable two-pad lumbar, 3D arms, headrest, 135° recline and leg rest.
- Mesh back for hot weather.
- Door delivery, COD, return window (Lazada up to 7 days, Shopee 15 days).
- High shop ratings (Lazada 5.0 from 8; Shopee 4.9 from 518).

## Cons
- Seat height, seat size, weight limit and chair weight not published.
- Warranty unclear.
- Most online praise is paid ads; the store asks buyers not to leave negative reviews.
- Owner-reported quirks: recline lock, lumbar slipping.
- Brand-level complaints about breakages and slow after-sales service.
- Must be assembled; can't be tried before buying.
