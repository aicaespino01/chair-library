# Okamura Feego mesh (CJ51BR FDM2), black with gray lumbar band, fixed polished arms, made 2016

**Identified: Confirmed.** The white Okamura label under the seat (photo sent 2026-11-26) gives product code **CJ51BR FDM2**, made **2016**, lot 22021612A. Japanese shops list CJ51BR as the **Feego mesh (フィーゴメッシュ) with polished design arms**. The manual card under the seat (photo sent earlier the same day) is the matching Okamura card. This file was first added as "Okamura black mesh chair, model not identified" and renamed once the label came in.

## At a glance
- Okamura Feego mesh: a mid-to-upper Okamura task chair (list price about ¥132,000 new), sold through the 2010s; now discontinued.
- **Seat-depth slide: yes**, 45 mm in 4 steps (maker manual). Ankle-tilt recline that locks, with tension knob.
- Polished aluminium base and polished arms; breathable mesh back; no PU or faux leather.
- Arms are fixed (no height adjust).
- Lowest seat about 40 cm (used shop measure, not the maker): close to her 40.6 cm popliteal height.
- **Made 2016**: past its 8-year JOIFA standard use period, so check the gas lift and recline carefully.

- CJ51BR = Feego mesh with polished design arms: ASKUL lists "オカムラ フィーゴメッシュチェア 固定肘付 CJ51BR"; KAGG lists "デザインアーム(ポリッシュ) CJ51BR-FDM2" under the Feego series (CJ41 = painted design arms, CJ91 = adjustable arms, CJ31 = no arms).

## Pros
- Okamura build; mesh back; no PU or faux leather.
- **Seat-depth slide** (45 mm).
- Lowest seat about 40 cm (shop measure): about right for a 150 cm user.
- Polished aluminium base and arms (sturdier than plastic).
- Used price in Japan is low (about ¥16,500–27,000), so the PH price should be modest too.

## Cons
- Made 2016, past its 8-year JOIFA standard use period.
- Fixed arms (no height adjust); may stop it sliding fully under a desk.
- No adjustable lumbar.
- Heavy (25.5 kg).
- Chrome-look polished parts may show corrosion in humid coastal air.
