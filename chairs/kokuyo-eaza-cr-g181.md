# Kokuyo EAZA (CR-G181 family): low-back Kokuyo task chair with fixed loop arms

**Identified: Very likely.** Google Lens on the listing photo returned Kokuyo EAZA pages (Kagg.jp CR-G181F6GET5-W and Office Busters Japan's used イーザチェア CRS-G182F6), and Google Images for CR-G181 shows the same low back, the same black loop arms and the same black 5-star base in the same colours. The Office Busters PH SKU is `KOKSWACGR1451`: `CGR` is Kokuyo's general code prefix CR-G, which fits CR-G181. **To confirm:** ask Office Busters for a photo of the label under the seat (it should read CR-G18x).

## At a glance
- Kokuyo office chair, low back, blue fabric, fixed black loop arms.
- Cheapest chair of this batch at ₱2,500, and the only one of the new batch with published maker sizes.
- Lowest seat 42.0 cm, which is normal office height, not a low-seat chair.
- Arms are fixed: they cannot be raised, lowered or moved out of the way.

## Pros
- Cheapest of this batch (₱2,500) and the price already dropped from ₱3,000.
- Real maker sizes are known, so you can check the fit before buying.
- Arms included, which most of the other cheap listings do not have.

## Cons
- Arms are fixed: no height or width adjust, and they may hit a low desk.
- Lowest seat 42.0 cm is a normal office height, so short users may still need a footrest.
- Recline, lumbar and weight limit are not published anywhere.
