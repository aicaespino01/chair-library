# Itoki Torino (KE-140CP family): 1999 Itoki task chair with the bending inner-shell seat

**Identified: Very likely.** Google Lens on the armless listing brought up Office Busters Japan's "トリノチェア" (Torino) used pages, and Itoki's own history page describes the Torino's "float bending seat". The two Office Busters PH listings carry the SKU hint `TRI` (`ITKSNATRI1483` no arms, `ITKSWATRI1453` loop arms), and their shape and light gray base match Torino photos. **To confirm:** ask Office Busters for a photo of the label under the seat (it should read KE-1xx).

## At a glance
- Older Itoki office chair (series launched 1999), fabric, low back, light gray base.
- Two units: light blue with no arms (in stock) and blue with fixed loop arms (sold out).
- Its selling point is the seat: a bending inner shell instead of a thick cushion, so it flexes with your weight.
- Itoki never published a seat height for it that could be found, so the lowest seat height is unknown. That matters if you are short.

## Pros
- Cheap for a real Japanese office chair (₱3,000).
- Synchro rocking with strength adjust and an angle lock, which budget new chairs rarely have.
- The bending seat shell does not flatten the way foam does, so an old unit may still feel firm.

## Cons
- No published seat height, seat size or weight limit, so you cannot check the fit before sitting on it.
- A 1999 design: gas lift and casters may be worn, and no spare parts here.
- No lumbar adjustment and no headrest.
- The loop-arm one is already sold out.
