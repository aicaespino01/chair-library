# Okamura Sulato (C331ZR): low back, no arms, black

**Identified: Very likely.** Found by a Google image search ("Okamura Sulato"). The Office Busters item code `OKASNASUL1522` decodes as Okamura, swivel with no arms, Sulato. The photos match Okamura's official C331ZR FSF1 photo: black shell, black plastic base, no arms, grip at the top of the back. Measuring the side photo fits the low back better than the high back. **To confirm:** ask Office Busters for a photo of the product label under the seat (it shows the code and year made).

## At a glance
- Okamura's lighter task chair, on sale in Japan since about 2014 and still sold new.
- Comfortable seat: slotted inner shell plus molded foam with 3 firmness zones (soft front, firm back).
- Smooth "ankle-tilt" recline (20°, locks anywhere, adjustable stiffness).
- No arms, no headrest, no lumbar adjustment, low back.
- Lowest seat (42.0 cm) is a bit high for people around 150 cm (Japanese seller's note).
- Used at Office Busters, so no Okamura warranty.

## Warranty and returns
- Okamura (Japan, per JOIFA): 1 year for finish and fabric wear, 2 years for mechanisms, 3 years for structure. It **does not cover** second-hand or transferred chairs, or use outside Japan. Repair parts are kept 5 years after a model ends. (Manual 2021-07)
- JOIFA standard use period for swivel chairs: 8 years at about 8 hours a day. This is a guide, not a warranty. (Catalog 2018)
- Office Busters: exchange only if proven defective **within 3 days** of purchase. No exchange for a lower-priced item. Free storage for 7 days after payment, then ₱500 a day. (OB storage and exchange policy)

## Shop claims not confirmed by the maker
- "Supports up to 100kg - 120kg": Okamura doesn't publish a weight limit.
- "Class 3 Gas Lift": Okamura doesn't publish the class.
- "5-Star Stable Nylon/Steel Base": the base is glass-fibre nylon; only the support under the seat is steel.
- "Mid-Back": Okamura calls this version **low back**.
