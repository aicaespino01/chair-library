# Itoki Spina (KE-757GP): elastomer back, adjustable arms, black base

**Identified: Confirmed.** A photo of the label under the seat shows the Itoki logo, **KE-757GPHT1T1SG**, lot **090331**, and JOIFA 602. The manual card in the seat pocket lists "スピーナチェアの機能・調節" (Spina chair functions). The ITOKI badge on the back post and the gray vertical-rib back match the Spina elastomer type.

## At a glance
- Itoki's high-function task chair. Won the Good Design award in 2007 (Itoki shop).
- Special back: gray "elastomer" ribs (soft, rubber-like plastic) that let air through, instead of mesh or cloth.
- Automatic lumbar and seat: when you sit, the seat slides back and the lower back pushes forward. No knobs needed.
- Adjustable arms (height and inward angle), 3-step recline limit.
- Lowest seat 40.0 cm (shop listing), good for shorter users.
- Expensive new in Japan (about ¥140,000); this unit looks like it was made in 2009 (lot 090331).

## How it was identified
- Label photo: "ITOKI / KE-757GPHT1T1SG / LOT. 090331C0083 / JOIFA602". Lot 090331 probably means made 31 March 2009.
- Shops sell **KE-757GP-T1T1SG** as "スピーナ エラストマータイプ ブラックベース アジャスタブル肘付" (elastomer type, black base, adjustable arms).
- Itoki's catalog color codes: base T1 = black, Z5 = silver metallic, Z9 = mirror aluminium; elastomer back/seat T1T1 = black, T1SG = smoke gray / black. So this chair: black base, smoke gray back, black seat.
- The extra "H" on the label (KE-757GP**H**…) is not in the shop codes. It may mean the hanger version (Itoki lists Spina with or without a hanger). Not confirmed.
- Two of the three photos show the ITOKI badge on the back post with the gray rib back behind it (same chair).

## Pros
- Automatic lumbar and seat slide: good posture without knobs.
- Breathable rib back, cooler than a cloth back.
- Adjustable arms and a 3-step recline limit.
- Low minimum seat height (40.0 cm, shop value).

## Cons
- Seat cushion may feel thin (1 shop review).
- No headrest, no manual seat-depth slide.
- This unit is about 17 years old (2009 lot); check the gas lift and the back ribs.
- Weight limit and chair weight not published.
