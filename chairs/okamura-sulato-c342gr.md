# Okamura Sulato (C342GR): low back, design arms, hanger, gray shell, black fabric

**Identified: Confirmed.** A clear photo of the label under the seat (sent by OfficeBusters PH, 2026-11-21) reads **C342GR FSF1**. Okamura's Sulato catalog lists C342GR as the low back with design arms and a hanger, gray shell, resin base, nylon casters. The shipping number on the label, **280521003**, also appears on the blurry label photo from 2026-11-14, so both photos show **the same chair unit**. That earlier photo was read as "C342BR"; the clear label shows the letter is **G**.

## At a glance
- Same Sulato seat and recline as the armless C331 (see the C331ZR file), plus fixed arms and a coat hanger.
- Comfortable seat with 3 firmness zones; 20° ankle-tilt recline that locks anywhere.
- Lowest seat 42.0 cm: a bit high for people around 150 cm.
- Made in 2021 (label); JOIFA standard service life 8 years, so roughly 3 of 8 years used.
- New in Japan about ¥90,800 (black shell version; gray shell price not found).
- OfficeBusters PH asked ₱?,000 in Facebook chat (first digit cut off in the screenshot). The armless Sulato C331ZR was ₱3,499 on their website.
