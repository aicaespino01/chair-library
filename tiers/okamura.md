# Okamura model tiers

A **tier** rates the **model (series)**, not one chair. It says how high the model sits in Okamura's own range: build, mechanism and what Okamura charged for it new. The condition and age of a chair, its price in the Philippines, its used price in Japan and the JOIFA standard use period never change the tier (JOIFA is 8 years for every swivel chair). The website's **value** rating is a separate question: whether the price asked is fair.

## How a tier is decided

Evidence is used in this order. The first one that settles it wins.

1. **Okamura's own class wording** (press release, catalog, product page), quoted in Japanese with the link.
2. **List price new for a comparable setup** (same back height, same arm type), with the year and source. Okamura has raised list prices over the years, so a 2008 launch price and a 2026 shop price can't be compared directly.
3. **Features and materials**, only to break ties: recline type (synchro with forward tilt > ankle-tilt > back rocking), seat-depth slide, lumbar, arm adjustment, base material, launch year.

**Confirmed** = settled by Okamura's own wording. **Provisional** = placed by price and features only; it can move if Okamura wording turns up.

## Tier scale

| Tier | Name | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Flagship | Okamura's top chairs: the most adjustment (often controls in the armrests), aluminium frames, highest list prices (about ¥120,000 to ¥260,000+ today). |
| 2 | Upper-mid | High-function task chairs: synchro recline with forward tilt, extra fit adjustments. |
| 3 | Middle | Okamura "middle class" (ミドルクラス): full basic adjustments (ankle-tilt recline that locks, seat slide, 3-density foam), better parts. |
| 4 | Upper standard | Okamura "one rank up standard" (ワンランク上のスタンダード): the best of the standard range, near-middle features. |
| 5 | Standard | Okamura's everyday office chairs: good ergonomics, mostly plastic parts, list prices about ¥45,000 to ¥90,000. |
| 6 | Entry | Budget and compact chairs: simpler recline (back rocking or basic synchro rocking), lowest list prices. |

## Notes

- **Feego vs Escudo (checked 26 Nov 2026):** Okamura calls the Feego mesh "middle class" and the Escudo a "standard model, one rank up", so Feego is one tier higher. The two share most of their parts: ankle-tilt recline, 92 mm height range, 3-density foam, 60 mm casters. Escudo's advantages are 5 mm more seat slide (50 vs 45 mm) and a newer flexing back. The Feego CJ51BR in the library also has a polished aluminium base and arms; the black Escudo C447ZR has a plastic base and arms.
- **Chairs not identified yet** get no tier until the model is known. The tier comes from the series in the chair file's model line.
- **Not placed yet:** Baron, Finora, CG-M and other Okamura series not in the library. Add a row when a chair of that series is added.
